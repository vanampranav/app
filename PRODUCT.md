# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fitness enthusiasts and health-conscious individuals — both FitDays smart-scale owners and
people without a scale. Primary jobs: track body composition (weight, body fat %, muscle mass),
log nutrition (calories, macros, meals), follow AI-generated workout & meal plans, and
understand long-term progress trends. Skill range: beginner tracking their first diet to
regular gym-goer optimising macros. Used daily in short bursts (log a meal, check the scale,
glance at the dashboard).

## Product Purpose

EleFit is a single-destination fitness companion: it connects a FitDays BLE smart scale, an
AI coach, a food log, and Apple Health / Health Connect into one coherent experience.
Success = the user understanding their body better every day and feeling progress, not just
seeing numbers.

## Brand Personality

Bold but friendly — Apple Fitness+ energy. Dark, polished, warm. Celebrates every milestone
without being juvenile. Three words: **Powerful. Precise. Encouraging.**

## Anti-references

- Pastel / soft-colour health apps — too gentle, clashes with the dark athletic aesthetic.
- Generic white health app UIs (old MyFitnessPal) — looks cheap and clinical.
- Cluttered medical dashboards — data overload kills motivation.
- Overly gamified apps with badges and confetti everywhere — feels juvenile.

## Design Principles

1. **Show progress, not just data** — every number needs visual context (ring, bar, trend line).
   Raw digits without scale feel meaningless.
2. **Dark is non-negotiable** — the dark surface is identity, not preference. Never introduce
   white or light-grey backgrounds.
3. **Lime earns attention** — the lime accent (#C8DA2B) is reserved for primary CTAs, active
   states, and key metrics only. Overusing it dilutes its power.
4. **Motion communicates, not decorates** — every animation should explain state change
   (loading → loaded, 0 → value, empty → filled). No gratuitous effects.
5. **Encourage, then inform** — lead with the motivational takeaway ("164 kcal · on track"),
   secondary with the raw data. Users feel first, analyse second.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large/UI elements).
- Support system dark-mode only (app is always dark).
- `preferReducedMotion`-aware animations — crossfade or instant fallback.
- Minimum 44×44 dp touch targets throughout.
