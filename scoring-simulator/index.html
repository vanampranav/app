How the scoring works

Show the rules

Each approved check-in adds points for improvement — points stack up and never go down (a poor week earns 0). The leaderboard ranks everyone by total points + admin bonus.

Weighted: Body-fat lost 50% · Weight lost 30% · Muscle gained 20%. Then points = max(0, weekly) × 10.

Anti-farming: improvement counts only beyond your best-so-far — yo-yoing to a weight you already hit earns nothing.
